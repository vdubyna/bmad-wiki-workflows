# Stage 5: Review And Handoff

Close with a registration summary and next steps.

## Present

Summarize:

- source registered;
- files created or updated;
- authority layer and status;
- privacy exclusions;
- unresolved questions;
- suggested linked concept or maintenance follow-up.

## Ask

Ask only the next decision needed:

- Should this source stay `draft` or be marked `active`?
- Should another source be registered now?
- Should maintenance scan use this source location as a future scan root?

## Do Not

- Do not approve a source without human review.
- Do not continue into broad maintenance scanning unless the user asks for it.

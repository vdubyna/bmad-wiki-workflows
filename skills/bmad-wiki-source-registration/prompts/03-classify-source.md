# Stage 3: Classify Source

Assign source type, authority, status, and registration intent.

## Authority Layers

- `canonical project docs`: source of truth for current project understanding.
- `current behavior`: code, tests, manifests, runtime docs, or live exported state.
- `working notes`: useful drafts, meeting notes, planning fragments, or incomplete decisions.
- `generated context`: AI output, generated summaries, or transformed artifacts.
- `external/raw context`: third-party references, copied research, transcripts, or URLs.

## Status Values

- `draft`;
- `active`;
- `superseded`;
- `archived`;
- `needs-review`.

## Continue When

You can explain why the source belongs in the wiki, what authority it carries, and what follow-up
questions remain.
